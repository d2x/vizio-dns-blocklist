! Title: Vizio SmartCast ads & telemetry blocklist
! Description: Blocks Vizio SmartCast home-screen ads, ad measurement, ACR (Inscape) viewing-data reporting, telemetry and Vizio's own content services (WatchFree+, catalogue/recommendation rows, voice search) while leaving the hosts that apps, Chromecast and firmware updates need.
! Homepage: https://github.com/d2x/vizio-dns-blocklist
! License: https://unlicense.org/
! Expires: 4 days
! Version: 2026.10.20.3
!
! Format: AdGuard / adblock-style DNS rules. `||host^` also blocks that host's subdomains.
! Deliberately NOT blocked (blocking these breaks the TV): api.vizio.com, scfs.vizio.com,
! osu.vudip.vizio.com (updates), vizio.pool.ntp.org (clock), *.plat.vizio.com (registration),
! user-authorization-prod.smartcasttv.com (sign-in), image-api-prod.smartcasttv.com (app artwork).
! Never block the whole vizio.com or smartcasttv.com zones.

! ---- ACR / viewing data (Inscape) ----
||tvinteractive.tv^
||inscape.tv^

! ---- Ads and ad measurement ----
||ads.vizio.com^
||vizio.adsmeasurement.com^
||admeasurement.com^
||impression-proxy-prod.smartcasttv.com^
||ad-broker-prod.smartcasttv.com^
||promotions-prod.smartcasttv.com^
||personalization-prod.smartcasttv.com^

! ---- Telemetry / logging / experiments ----
||al-smetrics.vizio.com^
||rlog4.vizio.com^
||rlog_sem.vizio.com^
||content-engagement-metrics.smartcasttv.com^
||experiments-prod.smartcasttv.com^
